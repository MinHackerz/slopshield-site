What SlopShield does

SlopShield reads the text shown on short videos in the apps you switch on, compares it with rules you choose, and swipes past videos that match. It works from text and labels only. It cannot see the video itself, so it will miss some AI-generated videos and will sometimes skip one that is not. Undo and Not AI exist for that reason.
